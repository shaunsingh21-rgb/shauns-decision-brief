# Privacy notice: AVL Tech Training & Equipment Hub (DRAFT)

> **DRAFT for review by the institution's counsel and privacy office before use. Not legal advice. Bracketed items [ ] must be completed by the institution. Wording is plain on purpose: it is meant to appear on the sign-in screen and on the Account page.**

**Who runs this:** [Department name], [Institution], IT Services. Questions or requests: [privacy contact / email].

**What this tool is for:** to record how AV equipment works, and to run the team's training: quizzes, practical scores and sign-offs.

**What it keeps about you**
* Your name, username, role, and when you last signed in.
* Your training record: quiz scores and attempts, practical scores and trainers' notes, and sign-offs (who signed you off, when, and any note).
* What you change in the tool (issues you post, equipment you edit or approve) and a log of actions (sign-ins, failed sign-ins, edits). The log includes the **network (IP) address** you used [for N days].
* If you turn on two-step sign-in: an encrypted code for your authenticator app.
* Photos or files you upload. Please do not upload photos of people. Please do not type other people's personal details (phone numbers, health, discipline) into issues or notes.

**What it does not do:** it does not track your location, record your screen, or read your email. It does not use advertising or analytics services.

**Who can see it**
* Everyone who is signed in can see the team list (names and roles), equipment, issues and training material.
* You see your own training record. Trainers and the team leader can see everyone's training progress.
* Only people the leader has authorised can see the activity log (including addresses) and the list of accounts.
* IT staff who run the server can see everything stored there, and backups.

**Outside services:** if your role allows "research" and "AI draft": the text you type into the web search box goes to **Brave Search**; text you paste into the AI draft box goes to **Anthropic**. Neither receives your name, username or address from this tool, but they do receive what you type. Do not type personal or student information there. **On your phone or laptop:** after you sign in, parts of the tool (including what you looked at) are saved on that device so it works offline. **Press Sign out when you finish on a shared or borrowed device**; this removes them. Always keep a screen lock. If you lose the device, tell [IT service desk] straight away.

**How long we keep it:** [see the retention schedule: e.g. training records for the length of your work with the team plus N years; activity log N months; addresses N days; backups N days].

**Your choices:** ask [contact] to see the information held about you, to correct it, or, when you leave, to have your name replaced with "Former team member" (training records are kept for the institution's records, without your name).
