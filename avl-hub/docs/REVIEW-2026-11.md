# Pre-launch review: ten reviewers, one app

Ten independent reviewers tested the running app in three teams. They reproduced problems rather than guessing, reported them with steps, and were told not to change anything. Every confirmed finding below was reproduced again, fixed, and given a test that fails without the fix. Tests: **155 API/security + 41 real-browser = 196.**

| Team | Reviewer | Verdict |
|---|---|---|
| Senior | Security architect | yes after fixes. Could not break permissions, CSRF, sessions, uploads, external calls. |
| Senior | Principal engineer | yes after fixes. No data loss or privilege problem. 10 of 10 deliberate breakages of key rules were caught by tests. |
| Senior | Product and accessibility lead | yes after fixes. Light-mode contrast clean on all screens; usability problems on phones. |
| Dev | Authentication pen-tester | yes after fixes. Two-step sign-in, sessions and CSRF held; lockout could be raced. |
| Dev | Authorization and injection pen-tester | yes after fixes. No escalation, XSS or SQL injection found. |
| Dev | Offline and sync engineer | yes after fixes. Conflict and replay logic held; identity and sign-out gaps. |
| Dev | Front-end QA | yes after fixes. No script errors anywhere; double-submit and cache-prefetch problems. |
| Client | AV technician (phone, patchy signal) | yes after fixes. A whole day of work succeeded, including offline. |
| Client | Trainer | yes after fixes. Rules firm and well explained; the record after edits was weak. |
| Client | Team leader / administrator | yes after fixes. Two real blockers and gaps in what an administrator can see. |

## Fixed (each with a test)

**Security and integrity**
* A change queued by one person could be sent under another person's name if they signed in on the same browser. Now refused, and sign-out wipes only the signing-out person's data. *(sync)*
* Restore did not refuse while the app was running and silently split the data. Now refuses. *(admin, sync, engineer)*
* Quiz answers could be worked out by trial with unlimited attempts. Now: all answers required, percentage only, attempt limit. *(security, authz)*
* Sign-in lockout could be raced with parallel guesses, locked by strangers, and was reset between code guesses. Redesigned. *(auth, security)*
* Import bypassed the permissions of four other kinds of content; export leaked quiz answers. *(authz)*
* The skill matrix bypassed "see everyone's progress". *(security)*
* Temporary passwords and recovery codes could be stored in clear in the replay table; replay ran before the permission check. *(three reviewers)*
* A malformed cookie or URL escape made every request, including sign-in, return an error. *(three reviewers)*
* A quiz score survived replacing the whole quiz; practical scores were ordered by arrival, not by when given. *(authz, sync)*
* Sessions never expired outright. A role that requires two-step was not enforced when the server lacked its key. A role manager could weaken the two-step requirement. *(security, auth)*
* Deleting equipment left its panels, modules link and updates live; a refused edit of a pin on a deleted panel still changed it. *(engineer)*
* Uploads were cut off only at the 200 MB video limit even for photos; leftovers from a crash were never swept; non-English file names were garbled. *(engineer, authz)*
* Concurrent same-name account creation gave a server error; backups were readable by anyone on the server; no graceful shutdown; helper scripts did not read `.env` under systemd. *(engineer, admin)*

**Sign-off record** *(trainer)*: sign-offs now remember the module version, scores and a note; show **out of date** after the material changes; can be **renewed**; re-scoring below the pass mark is flagged; messages say what to do ("no quiz taken yet", "add at least one question…"); the audit log records names, versions and scores.

**Blockers for administrators** *(admin)*: a new person in a two-step role could never finish (password change and setup waited on each other); the off-server backup folder did not work under Docker as documented; the Admin screens now show who is locked out, last sign-in, backup age, and a readable audit log.

**Ease of use and looks** *(UX, QA, technician)*: the background prefetch no longer exhausts the request limit (once per 10 minutes; limit is per person); a compact sticky header on phones; the People and audit tables become cards on phones; installation steps come right after the specs; pin placement shows where the pin will go, scrolls to the form, and pins show a source letter, not tint alone; double-taps cannot create duplicates; a finished sync never wipes a form being typed in; roles that cannot do something are not shown a way to try; long pasted links no longer stretch the page; page titles, a working skip link and focus after Next/Back; dark-mode contrast; toasts no longer stack; plain wording instead of environment variable names and "item(s)".

## Not fixed (deliberately or for later)
* **Module screen is one long page** mixing learner and trainer content. A tabbed layout (Learn / Quiz / Author / Sign-off) is the next usability step.
* **Skill matrix "who still needs what"** column (per-module quiz and skill gaps) and a trainees-only filter.
* **Video checkpoint times are not checked against the video length** (an author can enter 1:39 on a 6-second clip).
* **Control and direction-override characters** are not stripped from all names (they are rendered as text, so this is cosmetic).
* **Client clock offset** is not saved between page loads (a cold offline start on a phone with a wrong clock stamps edits with that clock; later and far-future times are clamped on the server).
* **No "update ready, reload" banner** after an app update beyond the existing toast; searching the equipment list loses the text after Back.
* **Corrupted local data** shows a plain "not saved yet" for that screen rather than repairing it.
* **Lockout can still be triggered by one address against one username** (5 tries, 15 minutes) by design; the real person from their own address is unaffected.
