# 30-minute live walkthrough (team of 6 to 8, everyone with their phone)

Room needs: projector or one shared phone mirrored, the pilot server address, accounts created beforehand, a table of printed quick starts, signal in the room (and one person walks to a dead spot for the offline demo, or use airplane mode).
Facilitator: the leader. Co-pilot: the trainer, who watches for people stuck.

| Min | Segment | Do (show, then they do) | Say / check |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0-3 | Why and what it is not | Slide or talk: what it is, what it is not, the one rule: **VERIFY means do not rely on it** | Read the announcement's "what it is not" aloud |
| 3-8 | Install and sign in | Everyone: open the address, Add to Home Screen, sign in with temporary password, choose own password | Co-pilot walks round; anyone stuck gets a leader reset after the session, not during |
| 8-13 | Find equipment | Search a named piece, open it, read the chips; find a VERIFY value | Ask: "What does Team mean? What would you do about VERIFY?" |
| 13-17 | Tick steps | Open the installation assistant, tick two steps, watch "[n] of [m] done" | Point out "not approved yet" banner and what it means |
| 17-21 | Log an issue | Issues & lessons, Log an issue or lesson, log "TEST" with equipment | Say: use a clear title; delete not available, so write test entries once |
| 21-26 | Offline | Airplane mode on: open the equipment you opened before, tick a step, log an issue; point to the amber bar; airplane mode off; tap the bar, Sync page | Check: everyone can name the message "Saved on this device". Say what is NOT saved offline (new pages, Account) |
| 26-28 | Training in two minutes | Show a module, the quiz, the skill matrix; trainer says how scoring works | "Quiz is marked when online. Sign-off needs 80% and every skill 2 or more." |
| 28-30 | Who to ask and next step | Write the contact on the board; the week-1 job for each person | Collect the one-line "what worries you" from each person |

Handouts: the technician quick start (B1); a card with four things: VERIFY = not confirmed; amber bar = saved on phone, will send; do not sign out with changes waiting; who to ask.

Do not demo: AI drafts, web research, the photo-label reading, video checkpoints (not needed in week 1; nothing in these was tested against real services).

Exit check (show of hands): signed in on own phone? icon on the home screen? can say what VERIFY means? If fewer than all yes, book 10 minutes one-to-one within 24 hours.
