# AVL Tech Hub: quick start for technicians (one page, phone)

Steps verified by doing them in a phone-sized browser (390x844). Replace [APP ADDRESS] with the address your team leader gives you.

## 1. Put it on your phone (once, with signal)
1. Open [APP ADDRESS] in Safari on iPhone (or your phone's browser).
2. iPhone: tap the Share button, then **Add to Home Screen**. The AVL Hub icon appears with your other apps. (Install wording differs by phone and was not tested on a real iPhone.)
3. Open it from the icon, not from the browser, from now on.

## 2. Sign in (once)
1. Your team leader gives you a **username** and a **temporary password** (they see it once; ask them to send it privately).
2. Sign in. The app asks you to **choose your own password**: at least 12 characters, not containing your username.
3. Two-step sign-in (a code from an authenticator app) is optional unless your team leader says your role needs it. You can turn it on later in **Account**.
4. Forgotten password? Ask your team leader to reset it. There is no email reset.

## 3. Find equipment
Tap **Equipment**. Type in **Search equipment**, tap the name. You see the specifications, where each value came from, the installation steps, photo panels and documents.
- The coloured chip is the **source**: **Manufacturer** (from the maker, with a web address), **Team** (your team wrote it), **AI-drafted** (a machine wrote it, needs a person to check), **VERIFY** (nobody has confirmed it).
- **VERIFY means do not rely on it.** The app never fills in a number it does not have. If a value is VERIFY, check the unit or the manual, then tell your team leader.

## 4. Tick installation steps
Open the equipment, scroll to **Installation assistant**, tick each step as you do it. The line under it shows "[n] of [m] done" and is saved to your account. If it says the steps are **not approved yet**, check them against the manual first.

## 5. Log an issue (or a lesson learned)
Tap **Issues & lessons**, then **Log an issue or lesson** (it opens a form). Choose the type, type a title, optionally pick the related equipment, tap the button. A good title says what and where: "Fader 3 crackles, Rack 2 mixer".

## 6. Work offline
- Open the app **once with signal** so it saves the equipment and training you have looked at. First visits to a page need signal.
- With no signal you can still read what you saw before, tick steps, log issues and answer quizzes. The top bar turns amber: **Offline, [n] changes waiting to sync**. A message says "Saved on this device".
- Quiz answers are marked when you are back online, not before. Your score then appears on the module.
- Back in signal the app sends your changes by itself. Tap the top bar to open the **Sync** page: it shows what is waiting, and **Sync now**.
- If someone else edited the same thing after you, their version is kept and you get a message. Open it and redo your change if you still need it.
- Pages you have never opened (for example **Account**) say you are offline until you open them once with signal.
- Do not sign out while changes are waiting: signing out throws them away (the app asks first).
- If you do not open the app for several weeks, iPhone can clear its saved data. Open it online now and then.

## 7. When something looks wrong
Note what you were doing and the message on screen, then tell your team leader. Do not retype a VERIFY value to make a warning go away.
